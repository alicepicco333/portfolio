Rijksmuseum Conversational AI Interface

A conversational interface that helps visitors learn about and engage with the Rijksmuseum collection inside the museum. It is the Data Systems Project of the MSc Information Studies (2026/27), with Q42 and Curamando as stakeholders.

Brief
Conversational interfaces are everywhere, but they rarely enrich a visit inside the museum.
Role
Designer and researcher in a team, MSc Information Studies Data Systems Project.
Method
Research into what makes a rich visit and which interface attributes support it, with Q42 and Curamando.

In progress (2026/27).

Notes

The brief asks what a visitor needs for a rich experience in the galleries and which interface attributes contribute to it, from input and output modalities to visual widgets and the context a conversation sits in, and how that experience can be measured.
