Archivio Associazione Luki Massa

Metadata for the digitized archive of Luki Massa, which documents lesbian and feminist activism from 1976 to 2015 and was inventoried by Emanuela Fiorletta in 2023. I turned the team's cataloguing spreadsheets and the scans into METS records for long-term preservation.

Brief
A digitized community archive that needed describing to national preservation standards.
Role
Metadata: the METS profile, the generation and validation pipeline, and the checks between catalogue and scans.
Method
Cataloguing spreadsheets and image files read by a Python pipeline into METS with EAD3, NISO MIX and METS Rights, validated against the official schemas.
Outcome
90 valid METS records describing 85 archival files, 1,629 documentary units and 4,942 pages.
Stack
Python, lxml, METS, EAD3, NISO MIX 2.0, XML Schema

Key decisions

  1. 01

    Describe only what has been scanned.

    Why
    The records are for the digital copies: a description without images would promise material that is not there.
    Result
    Four series are described; the one not yet digitized can be added when its scans exist.
  2. 02

    Take sizes, checksums and image data from the files themselves, not from the spreadsheets.

    Why
    Preservation depends on knowing that each file is exactly the one described.
    Result
    Every one of the 9,882 images has a verified MD5 checksum and its real technical data.
  3. 03

    Report mismatches between catalogue and scans to the team instead of patching them in the records.

    Why
    The spreadsheets are the archive's catalogue, so corrections belong there.
    Result
    The team corrected names and rows, and the records were regenerated from the corrected catalogue.
  4. 04

    Three levels of records: per archival file, per series and for the whole archive.

    Why
    Each folder carries its own record, while the series and archive records give the whole structure in one place.
    Result
    All three levels link exactly the same 9,882 images, checked automatically.

Notes

The records follow the Italian national guidelines for digitizing cultural heritage (ICDP, 2022): METS as the container, EAD3 for the archival description from the whole archive down to each page, NISO MIX for the technical data of every image and METS Rights for the rights. A Python pipeline reads the spreadsheets and the image files themselves, so every size and MD5 checksum comes from the real file, and validates each record against the official schemas. Comparing the sheets with the scans also turned up pages listed without a file, files without a row and names that did not match; the team corrected them and the records were rebuilt from the corrected catalogue.

The hand-in is 90 METS files at three levels: one per archival file, one per series and one for the whole archive, each stored next to the images it describes, with every link checked against the archive's storage. Only what has been scanned is described: four series, from political and cultural activism to Luki Massa's personal papers and posters.
