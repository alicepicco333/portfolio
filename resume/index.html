HCI researcher & designer · user research, accessibility, interaction design, data visualization

I'm an HCI researcher and designer based in Amsterdam. I start from how people actually use technology, test ideas with them, and turn what I learn into clear, inclusive interfaces, data visualizations and working prototypes. My background is in cultural anthropology and digital humanities; I'm now doing an MSc in Information Studies (Human-Computer Interaction) at the University of Amsterdam.
