Drive Junior

Junior is a probe rover for salvage workers on Proxima b, where a glowing fungus marks buried hazards. You are Marvin, the worker: you send Junior ahead with a squeeze, your hand and your voice, so you never walk up to a hazard yourself.

A first run

  1. Squeeze (V). Junior stops, his camera fills your visor, and he reports a possible hazard.
  2. Drive a little closer with the arrow keys or the pad. Stay about a metre from the glows: no ticks yet.
  3. Point at a glowing patch with a click or a tap, then say "Junior, check that one" or press Check that one. He drives there, goes round rocks and stops about 30 cm in front. The ticks speed up as he gets closer.
  4. Say "Junior, come back" or press Come back. He turns and drives straight home.

Controls

Squeeze V
The pressure sensor in the glove's palm. It swaps which camera fills your visor and stops Junior first.
Hand ←↑→↓ X
Up is an open hand (drive), left and right tilt it (steer), down is a thumb down (reverse), X is a fist (stop). WASD and the pad work too. Your hand reads your own gestures on camera; a hand held in view for 0.6 s takes over from Junior.
Voice hold Space
Say "Junior, check that one", "Junior, come back", "stop", "left" or "right". Works in Chrome, Edge and Safari; the phrase buttons do the same everywhere.
Point click or tap
Stands in for pointing with your finger. With two glows in view and no pointing, Junior asks which one.
Flare F
A burst of light. Junior stops, ignores what his camera sees and refuses voice orders to drive until it fades.

No language model here: in the prototype a local model could suggest which glow you meant, but fixed rules made every decision to move. This version keeps the rules. Nothing is recorded. The camera picture stays in your browser; speech recognition is done by your browser, which may send the audio to its maker.
